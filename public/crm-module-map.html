Rules applied everywhere

  • Two levels at most. "Product Management › Offers › Single Offers" became "Catalogue › Products & Plans".
  • Reference lists live in Settings. Low-volume setup pages no longer get top-level menu slots.
  • Breadcrumbs replace Go Back / Go Forward. They are built from the menu, so every page shows where it sits.
  • Risky actions sit in the ⋯ menu. Suspend, publish, revoke and deactivate ask for confirmation, and most need a reason.
  • Status is read-only in lists. No one-click suspend or publish from a table row.
  • Deactivate, never delete for users, and archive instead of delete for plans that are in use.
  • Names match the business. Sales Channels are Partners; Product Captions are Features; Marketing Channels are really lead sources.
  • Shortcuts. Star any page or quick action as a favorite; the avatar menu has My profile and Log out.
